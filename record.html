Word recorder

Record each Bosnian word once, clearly and naturally, one speaker for all of them. Saved recordings go straight into assets/audio/words/ and the game uses them instead of the browser voice.
Space start / stop recording · P play it back · Enter save and go to the next word · ↑↓ choose a word
The Space presses at the start and end are cut off by themselves, and the volume is evened out. Wait a moment after pressing Space before you speak, and pause briefly after the word before pressing it again.
